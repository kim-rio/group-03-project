Rio de Janeiro, Brazil 🇧🇷

Cidade Maravilhosa (The Marvelous City)

4.6 Rating Tropical Samba

Overview

Rio de Janeiro is a huge seaside city in Brazil, famed for its Copacabana and Ipanema beaches, 38m Christ the Redeemer statue atop Mount Corcovado and for Sugarloaf Mountain, a granite peak with cable cars to its summit. The city is also known for its sprawling favelas (shanty towns). Its raucous Carnaval festival, featuring parade floats, flamboyant costumes and samba dancers, is considered the world's largest.

Food & Dining

Rio's cuisine reflects Brazil's diverse cultural influences:

🥩 Brazilian BBQ

Rodízio-style churrascarias with endless meat selections.

🥘 Feijoada

Traditional black bean stew with pork, served on Saturdays.

🍹 Caipirinha

National cocktail made with cachaça, lime, and sugar.

🍮 Brigadeiro

Chocolate truffle balls made with condensed milk.

Travel Tips

Sun Protection

Tropical sun is intense; use high SPF and reapply often.

Beach Safety

Only bring essentials to the beach and watch your belongings.

Carry Cash

Many small establishments and vendors only accept cash.

Public Transport

Use Uber or official taxis; avoid unmarked vehicles.
