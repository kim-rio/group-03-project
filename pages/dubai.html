Dubai, UAE

City of Superlatives and Innovation

4.8 Rating Warm Shopping

Overview

Dubai is a city and emirate in the United Arab Emirates known for luxury shopping, ultramodern architecture and a lively nightlife scene. Burj Khalifa, an 830m-tall tower, dominates the skyscraper-filled skyline. At its foot lies Dubai Fountain, with jets and lights choreographed to music.

Population

3.4 Million

Best Time to Visit

November - March

Language

Arabic

Currency

UAE Dirham (AED)

Top Attractions

Burj Khalifa

World's tallest building with observation decks on 124th and 148th floors.

2-3 hours AED 169

The Dubai Mall

World's largest shopping mall with aquarium, ice rink, and waterfall.

4-6 hours Free entry

Desert Safari

Dune bashing, camel rides, and Bedouin-style dinner under stars.

6-8 hours AED 250

Food & Dining

Dubai's culinary scene reflects its multicultural population.

Arabic Mezze

Hummus, tabbouleh, falafel, and grilled meats.

Arabic Coffee

Traditional gahwa with cardamom, served with dates.

Shawarma

Marinated meat roasted on a spit, served in pita bread.

International Cuisine

World-class restaurants by celebrity chefs.

Travel Tips

Dress Modestly

Cover shoulders and knees in public places and malls.

Transport

Use metro, taxis, or ride-hailing apps; avoid driving.

Heat Management

Stay hydrated and plan outdoor activities for morning/evening.

Shopping

Visit during Dubai Shopping Festival (Dec-Feb) for deals.

Quick Facts

  • Timezone: GST (UTC+4)
  • Emergency: 999
  • Voltage: 220V/50Hz
  • Tipping: 10-15%
  • VAT: 5%

Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis, JBR Beach
