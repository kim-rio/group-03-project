Tokyo, Japan 🇯🇵

Where Ancient Traditions Meet Neon Future

4.9 Rating Efficient Transit 24/7 Energy

Overview

Tokyo, Japan's bustling capital, mixes ultramodern and traditional, from neon-lit skyscrapers to historic temples. The city is known for its vibrant pop culture, incredible cuisine, and efficient public transportation. Experience everything from ancient shrines in Asakusa to cutting-edge technology in Akihabara.

Food & Dining

Tokyo has more Michelin stars than any other city. Must-try foods include:

🍣 Sushi

Fresh nigiri at Tsukiji Market or high-end omakase experiences.

🍜 Ramen

Rich tonkotsu or shoyu broth with perfectly cooked noodles.

🥩 Wagyu Beef

Premium Japanese beef known for intense marbling and flavor.

🍵 Matcha

Traditional green tea and matcha-flavored desserts.

Recommended: Sukiyabashi Jiro

Ginza Station

Travel Tips

Suica Card

Get a rechargeable IC card for all public transportation.

Onsen Etiquette

Shower thoroughly before entering public baths.

Cash is King

Many places don't accept credit cards, carry sufficient cash.

Walking Shoes

You'll walk 10-20k steps daily exploring different districts.
