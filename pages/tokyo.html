Tokyo, Japan

Where Ancient Traditions Meet the Future

4.9 Rating Efficient Transit 24/7 Energy

Overview

Tokyo, Japan's bustling capital, mixes ultramodern and traditional, from neon-lit skyscrapers to historic temples. The city is known for its vibrant pop culture, incredible cuisine, and efficient public transportation.

Population

13.9 Million

Best Time to Visit

March - May, September - November

Language

Japanese

Currency

Yen (¥)

Top Attractions

Shibuya Crossing

World's busiest pedestrian crossing with thousands crossing simultaneously.

1-2 hours Free

Senso-ji Temple

Tokyo's oldest temple with well and attractive landscape

2-3 hours Free

Tokyo Skytree

World's tallest tower offering panoramic views of the Kanto region.

2-3 hours ¥3,100

Food & Dining

Tokyo has more Michelin stars than any other city. The food scene ranges from street food to world-class dining.

Sushi

Fresh nigiri at Tsukiji Market or high-end omakase experiences.

Ramen

Rich tonkotsu or shoyu broth with perfectly cooked noodles.

Wagyu Beef

Premium Japanese beef known for intense marbling and flavor.

Matcha

Traditional green tea and matcha-flavored desserts.

Travel Tips

Suica Card

Get a rechargeable IC card for all public transportation.

Onsen Etiquette

Shower thoroughly before entering public baths.

Cash is King

Many places don't accept credit cards, carry sufficient cash.

Walking Shoes

You'll walk 10-20k steps daily exploring different districts.

Quick Facts

  • Timezone: JST (UTC+9)
  • Emergency: 110 (Police)
  • Voltage: 100V/50-60Hz
  • Tipping: Not customary
  • Wi-Fi: Excellent city-wide

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Modern Tokyo

Shibuya, Harajuku, Shinjuku

Day 2: Historical Tokyo

Asakusa, Ueno Park, Akihabara

Day 3: Coastal Tokyo

Tsukiji Market, Odaiba, Tokyo Tower
