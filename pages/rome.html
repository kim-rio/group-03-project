Rome, Italy 🇮🇹

The Eternal City

4.8 Rating Ancient Walkable

Overview

Rome is the capital city of Italy. It is also the capital of the Lazio region, the centre of the Metropolitan City of Rome, and a special comune named Comune di Roma Capitale. Rome is located in the central-western portion of the Italian Peninsula, within Lazio (Latium), along the shores of the Tiber. Vatican City (the smallest country in the world) is an independent country inside the city boundaries of Rome, the only existing example of a country within a city.

Food & Dining

Roman cuisine is simple, seasonal, and delicious:

🍝 Carbonara

Pasta with eggs, Pecorino Romano, guanciale, and black pepper.

🍕 Roman Pizza

Thin, crispy crust pizza al taglio (by the slice).

🧀 Suppli

Fried rice balls with tomato sauce and mozzarella.

🍷 Italian Wine

Excellent local wines from Lazio region.

Travel Tips

Skip-the-Line Tickets

Book online for Colosseum, Vatican, and other major sites.

Comfortable Shoes

Rome is best explored on foot; prepare for cobblestone streets.

Water Fountains

Free drinking water from "nasoni" fountains throughout the city.

Meal Times

Lunch: 1-3 PM, Dinner: 8-11 PM; many restaurants close in afternoon.
