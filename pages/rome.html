Rome, Italy

The Eternal City

4.8 Rating Ancient Walkable

Overview

Rome is the capital city of Italy. It is also the capital of the Lazio region, the centre of the Metropolitan City of Rome. Rome is located in the central-western portion of the Italian Peninsula, within Lazio, along the shores of the Tiber. Vatican City is an independent country inside the city boundaries of Rome. You will feel the past, present and the future simultaneously. Welcome to Rome we like to see you here.

Population

2.8 Million

Best Time to Visit

April - June, September - October

Language

Italian

Currency

Euro (€)

Top Attractions

Colosseum

Ancient amphitheater where gladiators fought, symbol of Imperial Rome.

2-3 hours €24

Vatican Museums

World's largest art collection including the Sistine Chapel.

4-6 hours €21

Trevi Fountain

Baroque masterpiece and Rome's largest fountain; throw a coin to return.

30-60 minutes Free

Food & Dining

Roman cuisine is simple, seasonal, and delicious with fresh ingredients.

Carbonara

Pasta with eggs, Pecorino Romano, guanciale, and black pepper.

Roman Pizza

Thin, crispy crust pizza al taglio (by the slice).

Suppli

Fried rice balls with tomato sauce and mozzarella.

Italian Wine

Excellent local wines from Lazio region.

Travel Tips

Skip-the-Line Tickets

Book online for Colosseum, Vatican, and other major sites.

Comfortable Shoes

Rome is best explored on foot; prepare for cobblestone streets.

Water Fountains

Free drinking water from "nasoni" fountains throughout the city.

Meal Times

Lunch: 1-3 PM, Dinner: 8-11 PM; many restaurants close in afternoon.
