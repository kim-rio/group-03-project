Cape Town, South Africa

Where Mountains Meet Oceans

4.8 Rating Year-round Sun Nature

Overview

Cape Town is a port city on South Africa's southwest coast, on a peninsula beneath the imposing Table Mountain. It's known for its harbor, natural setting, and landmarks like Table Mountain and Cape Point.

Population

4.6 Million

Best Time to Visit

March - May, September - November

Language

English, Afrikaans

Currency

South African Rand (R)

Top Attractions

Table Mountain

Flat-topped mountain forming a dramatic backdrop to Cape Town.

3-4 hours R380

Robben Island

UNESCO site where Nelson Mandela was imprisoned for 18 years.

4 hours R600

Cape Point

Dramatic cliffs at the meeting point of Atlantic and Indian Oceans.

4-5 hours R360

Food & Dining

Cape Town offers incredible cuisine influenced by Malay, Dutch, and African traditions.

Braai

South African barbecue with boerewors and sosaties.

Bobotie

Spiced minced meat baked with egg-based topping.

Cape Wine

World-class wines from Stellenbosch and Franschhoek.

Seafood

Fresh crayfish, oysters, and line fish from local waters.

Travel Tips

Car Rental

Rent a car to explore the Cape Peninsula and Winelands.

Sun Protection

African sun is intense; use SPF 50+ and wear hats.

Cash & Cards

Most places accept cards, but carry cash for markets.

Book in Advance

Popular attractions sell out; book Table Mountain tickets early.

Quick Facts

  • Timezone: SAST (UTC+2)
  • Emergency: 10111
  • Voltage: 230V/50Hz
  • Tipping: 10-15%
  • VAT: 15%

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: City & Mountain

Table Mountain, V&A Waterfront, Robben Island

Day 2: Peninsula Tour

Chapman's Peak, Boulders Beach, Cape Point

Day 3: Wine & Culture

Stellenbosch, Franschhoek, District Six Museum
