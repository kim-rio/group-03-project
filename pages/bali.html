Bali, Indonesia 🇮🇩

Island of Gods and Tropical Paradise

4.9 Rating Tropical Wellness

Overview

Bali is a province of Indonesia and the westernmost of the Lesser Sunda Islands. East of Java and west of Lombok, the province includes the island of Bali and a few smaller neighbouring islands, notably Nusa Penida, Nusa Lembongan, and Nusa Ceningan. It is renowned for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches and coral reefs. The island is home to religious sites such as cliffside Uluwatu Temple.

Food & Dining

Balinese cuisine is flavorful and aromatic:

🍛 Nasi Goreng

Indonesian fried rice with chicken, vegetables, and fried egg.

🍢 Sate Lilit

Minced seafood satay wrapped around lemongrass sticks.

🥥 Babi Guling

Spit-roasted pig with crispy skin, a Balinese specialty.

🥭 Fresh Tropical Fruit

Mango, dragon fruit, rambutan, and salak (snake fruit).

Travel Tips

Scooter Rental

Best way to explore; wear helmets and get international license.

Water Safety

Drink only bottled water; avoid ice in questionable places.

Temple Etiquette

Wear sarong and sash when entering temples.

Cash & Bargaining

Carry cash; bargain politely in markets (start at 50%).
