Bali, Indonesia

Island of Gods and Tropical Paradise

4.9 Rating Tropical Wellness

Overview

Bali is a province of Indonesia and the westernmost of the Lesser Sunda Islands. It is renowned for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches and coral reefs. The island is home to religious sites such as cliffside Uluwatu Temple.

Population

4.3 Million

Best Time to Visit

April - October (Dry Season)

Language

Indonesian, Balinese

Currency

Indonesian Rupiah (IDR)

Top Attractions

Uluwatu Temple

Sea temple perched on a cliff with stunning sunset views and Kecak dance.

2-3 hours IDR 50,000

Tegalalang Rice Terrace

Iconic terraced rice fields with dramatic green landscapes.

2-3 hours IDR 25,000

Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

Active volcano with breathtaking sunrise views from the summit.

6-8 hours IDR 400,000

Food & Dining

Balinese cuisine is flavorful and aromatic with rich spices.

Nasi Goreng

Indonesian fried rice with chicken, vegetables, and fried egg.

Sate Lilit

Minced seafood satay wrapped around lemongrass sticks.

Babi Guling

Spit-roasted pig with crispy skin, a Balinese specialty.

Fresh Tropical Fruit

Mango, dragon fruit, rambutan, and salak (snake fruit).

Travel Tips

Scooter Rental

Best way to explore; wear helmets and get international license.

Water Safety

Drink only bottled water; avoid ice in questionable places.

Temple Etiquette

Wear sarong and sash when entering temples.

Cash & Bargaining

Carry cash; bargain politely in markets (start at 50%).

Quick Facts

  • Timezone: WITA (UTC+8)
  • Emergency: 112
  • Voltage: 230V/50Hz
  • Tipping: Not expected
  • Visa: Free for 30 days

3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Ubud Culture

Monkey Forest, Rice Terraces, Traditional Dance

Day 2: Temples & Beaches

Uluwatu, Jimbaran Bay, Kecak Fire Dance

Day 3: Volcano & Waterfalls

Mount Batur, Tirta Empul, Tegenungan Waterfall
