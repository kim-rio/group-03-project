Paris, France

The City of Light and Love

4.8 Rating Family Friendly Walkable City

Overview

Paris, France's capital, is a major city and a global center for art, fashion, cultu

Population

2.1 Million

Best Time to Visit

April - June, September - October

Language

French

Currency

Euro (€)

Top Attractions

Eiffel Tower

Iconic iron lattice tower offering breathtaking city views from its observation decks.

2-3 hours €25-€50

Louvre Museum

World's largest art museum and historic monument, home to Mona Lisa.

4-6 hours €17

Notre-Dame Cathedral

Medieval Catholic cathedral known for its French Gothic architecture.

1-2 hours Free

Food & Dining

Parisian cuisine is world-renowned with both traditional and modern influences.

Croissants

Flaky, buttery pastries best enjoyed fresh from a boulangerie.

French Cheese

Over 400 varieties including Brie, Camembert, and Roquefort.

French Wine

World-class wines from regions like Bordeaux and Burgundy.

Fine Dining

Michelin-starred restaurants offering exquisite French cuisine.

Travel Tips

Public Transport

Get a Paris Visite travel card for unlimited metro, bus, and RER travel.

Language

Learn basic French phrases. Locals appreciate "Bonjour" and "Merci".

Budget Tips

Visit museums on first Sundays of the month for free entry.

Walking

Paris is best explored on foot. Wear comfortable shoes!

Quick Facts

  • Timezone: CET (UTC+1)
  • Emergency: 112
  • Voltage: 230V/50Hz
  • Tipping: 10-15% in restaurants
  • Wi-Fi: Available in most cafes

Day 2: Historical Heart

Notre-Dame, Latin Quarter, Pantheon

Day 3: Artistic Side

Montmartre, Sacré-Cœur, Moulin Rouge
