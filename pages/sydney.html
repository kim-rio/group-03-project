Sydney, Australia

Harbor City with Iconic Beaches

4.7 Rating Harbor City Outdoor Lifestyle

Overview

Sydney, capital of New South Wales and one of Australia's largest cities, is best known for its harbourfront Sydney Opera House, with a distinctive sail-like design. Massive Darling Harbour and the smaller Circular Quay port are hubs of waterside life.

Population

5.3 Million

Best Time to Visit

September - November, March - May

Language

English

Currency

Australian Dollar (A$)

Top Attractions

Sydney Opera House

UNESCO World Heritage site and iconic performing arts venue.

2-3 hours A$42

Sydney Harbour Bridge

Climb the iconic "Coathanger" for panoramic harbor views.

3-4 hours A$308

Bondi Beach

World-famous beach with great surf, cafes, and coastal walks.

2-6 hours Free

Food & Dining

Sydney's food scene reflects its multicultural population and coastal location.

Meat Pie

Australian classic with minced meat and gravy in flaky pastry.

Sydney Rock Oysters

Fresh oysters from the pristine waters around Sydney.

Hunter Valley Wine

World-class Semillon and Shiraz from nearby wine region.

Modern Australian

Fusion cuisine combining Asian and European influences.

Travel Tips

Opal Card

Get a rechargeable Opal card for all public transport.

Sun Safety

Australian sun is strong; wear SPF 50+, hat, and sunglasses.

Beach Safety

Swim between red and yellow flags at patrolled beaches.

Coastal Walks

Walk from Bondi to Coogee for stunning coastal views.
