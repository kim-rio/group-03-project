New York City, USA 🇺🇸

The City That Never Sleeps

4.7 Rating 24/7 Subway Diverse

Overview

New York City comprises 5 boroughs sitting where the Hudson River meets the Atlantic Ocean. At its core is Manhattan, a densely populated borough that's among the world's major commercial, financial and cultural centers. Its iconic sites include skyscrapers such as the Empire State Building and sprawling Central Park.

Travel Tips

MetroCard

Get unlimited 7-day MetroCard for subway and bus access.

Walk or Subway

Traffic is terrible; walking or subway is faster than taxis.

Broadway Tickets

Get discounted same-day tickets at TKTS booths.

Dress in Layers

Weather changes quickly; subway stations can be hot/cold.
