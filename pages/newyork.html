New York City, USA

The City That never rest

4.8 Rating 24/7 Subway Diverse

Overview

New York City comprises 5 boroughs sitting where the Hudson River meets the Atlantic Ocean. At its core is Manhattan, a densely populated borough that's among the world's major commercial, financial and cultural centers. Its iconic sites include skyscrapers such as the Empire State Building and sprawling Central Park.

Population

8.4 Million people

Best Time to Visit the city

April - June, September - November

Language

English

Currency

US Dollar ($)

Top Attractions

Statue of Liberty

Iconic symbol of freedom and democracy on Liberty Island.

3-4 hours $24.50

Central Park

843-acre urban park in Manhattan with lakes, trails, and attractions.

2-6 hours Free

Times Square

Bustling commercial intersection known for bright neon signs.

1-2 hours Free

Food & Dining

NYC is a culinary melting pot with influences from around the world.

NYC Pizza

Thin crust, foldable slices from legendary pizzerias.

Street Hot Dogs

Classic NYC street food with sauerkraut and mustard.

Bagels

Fresh, chewy bagels with cream cheese and lox.

Pastrami Sandwiches

Katz's Delicatessen-style piled-high sandwiches.

Travel Tips

MetroCard

Get unlimited 7-day MetroCard for subway and bus access.

Walk or Subway

Traffic is terrible; walking or subway is faster than taxis.

Broadway Tickets

Get discounted same-day tickets at TKTS booths.

Dress in Layers

Weather changes quickly; subway stations can be hot/cold.

Quick Facts

  • Timezone: EST (UTC-5)
  • Emergency: 911
  • Voltage: 120V/60Hz
  • Tipping: 15-20% standard
  • Sales Tax: 8.875%
